In older septic patients, baseline fragility matters. Advanced age, higher comorbidity burden, and poorer nutritional reserve reduce physiologic resilience when acute organ dysfunction develops. GNRI, BMI, and comorbidity indices do not determine outcome alone, but they change how the same SOFA or hemodynamic insult should be interpreted. An elderly patient with multi-organ stress and low reserve should be reasoned about more cautiously than a younger, less comorbid patient.
