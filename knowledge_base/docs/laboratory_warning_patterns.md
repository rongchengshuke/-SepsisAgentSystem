Prognostic interpretation in septic ICU windows should pay attention to clusters of abnormalities. Elevated lactate, worsening creatinine, hyperbilirubinemia, thrombocytopenia, leukocyte abnormalities, acidosis, and poor oxygenation often point toward multi-organ stress rather than a single isolated issue. The reasoning report should describe which systems look threatened and whether the current pattern suggests stabilization or progression. Laboratory signals become more concerning when they align with vasopressor need or reduced urine output.
